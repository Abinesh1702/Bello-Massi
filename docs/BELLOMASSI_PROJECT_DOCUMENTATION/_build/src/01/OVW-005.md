## 1. Benefits for Bellomassi — from SOW slide 18

| Benefit | Description | Measurable after go-live (proposed KPI) |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Nothing ships without manager approval and a credit check | % orders approved before dispatch |
| Visibility | One live status for every order | Status enquiries between teams |
| One record | Same order flows from sales to delivery | Re-entry errors |
| Efficient delivery | Zone planning and fastest route | Stops per driver per day; km per stop |
| Accountability | Time-stamped trail | Audit completeness |
| Ready for SAP | Structured data from day one | SAP mapping effort |

## 2. Benefits for customers — from SOW slide 18

Orders captured accurately · faster decisions · deliveries planned the day before · secure OTP handover · instant paperwork.

## 3. Additional benefits from prototype extensions — PENDING CONFIRMATION of scope

Stock accuracy per hub and controlled write-offs (inventory, scrap) · no non-compliant vehicles or drivers on the road (VMS) ·
central verified documents (documents / OCR) · customer self-service ordering and tracking (customer app) · demand
forecasts and reorder suggestions (AI).
